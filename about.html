Our story

Locally based, personally run

Diganta Travels Pvt. Ltd. is an Andaman travel company based in Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair). We plan holidays the way we would for our own families — with the right hotels, the right ferry timings and enough slack in the day for one more sunset.

Over seventeen years we’ve learned the language of these islands: the tides that decide when you can see the Natural Bridge, the convoy times for Baratang, which cruise is fastest in the afternoon and which beach is quietest in the morning. That local knowledge is what you’re really booking.

Every itinerary is written for you, every hotel is one we have checked in person, and our team stays reachable 24 hours a day while you travel.
