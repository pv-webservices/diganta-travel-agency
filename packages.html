Good to know

Package FAQs

What is included in the package price?

Packages are quoted for your dates, hotel category and group size. The written quotation lists every inclusion — typically hotels with breakfast, private cruise tickets, AC cab transfers, sightseeing and airport pick-up and drop.

Are water activities included?

Scuba, sea walk, snorkelling and glass-bottom rides are usually paid on the spot unless your quotation says otherwise. Ask us to pre-book them — slots fill fast in season.

Can I change a package?

Always. Lengthen it, shorten it, combine two, add Baratang, an extra Havelock night or a Diglipur extension. Every itinerary here is a starting point.

How many days do I need for the Andamans?

Five to seven days covers Sri Vijaya Puram, Swaraj Dweep and Shaheed Dweep comfortably. Add two to four days for Baratang, Rangat or Diglipur.

Do you book flights?

We can suggest the best flight timings for your itinerary and help with bookings on request. Flights are not part of the standard package price.

What should I check before booking?

Confirm the itinerary, hotel names, total price, payment schedule and the amendment and cancellation terms in writing. We share all of these before you pay anything.
