Chapter 02The Crossing

Six islands.
One unbroken journey.

01 / 06 · Arrive by air

Sri Vijaya Puram

formerly Port Blair · 11.62° N 92.72° E

Where every Diganta journey begins. We meet you at the airport and ease you in — Cellular Jail’s light-and-sound show, Corbyn’s Cove, and boat trips to Ross Island and North Bay.

  • Cellular Jail
  • Ross Island
  • North Bay
  • Chidiya Tapu

02 / 06 · ≈ 2 hrs by cruise

Swaraj Dweep

formerly Havelock · 11.98° N 92.96° E

Powder-white sand and water in impossible blues. Sunset at Radhanagar, snorkelling at Elephant Beach, and the islands’ best dive sites just offshore.

  • Radhanagar Beach
  • Elephant Beach
  • Kalapathar
  • Scuba

03 / 06 · ≈ 1 hr from Swaraj Dweep

Shaheed Dweep

formerly Neil Island · 11.83° N 93.02° E

The slow island. Cycle between villages, walk under the Natural Rock bridge at low tide, and watch the sun rise over Sitapur.

  • Natural Bridge
  • Laxmanpur
  • Bharatpur
  • Sitapur

04 / 06 · Road & creek safari

Baratang

Middle Andaman · 12.12° N 92.78° E

Speedboats through tunnels of mangroves, a jungle walk to ancient limestone caves, bubbling mud volcanoes and a dusk full of parrots.

  • Limestone Caves
  • Mud Volcano
  • Parrot Island

05 / 06 · Day voyage by sea

Barren Island

Andaman Sea · 12.27° N 93.85° E

India’s only active volcano, rising straight out of the deep sea. Seen from the deck — smoke on the summit, black lava slopes, ink-blue water.

  • Active Volcano
  • Deep-sea views
  • Sail-by

06 / 06 · The far north

Diglipur

North Andaman · 13.26° N 93.01° E

The edge of the map. Twin islands of Ross & Smith joined by a sandbar, the climb up Saddle Peak, and turtle-nesting beaches.

  • Ross & Smith
  • Saddle Peak
  • Kalipur
