Before your island escape

The practical side of an Andaman holiday — when to go, how to get there, what to pack and what to expect.

Best time to visit

Weather through the year

The islands are warm all year (23–31 °C). What changes is the sea: calm, clear water makes the best months for ferries and diving.

October – February Peak season

Pleasant days, calm seas and the best visibility for scuba and snorkelling. Ideal for honeymoons and families. Book cruises and hotels early, especially around Christmas and New Year.

March – May Summer

Hotter and more humid, but the sea is still calm and dive conditions are excellent. Fewer crowds and better hotel availability.

June – September Monsoon

Heavy showers and rougher seas; some water activities and ferries may be suspended. Lush green islands and the lowest prices for flexible travellers.

Getting here

How to reach the Andamans

By air

Veer Savarkar International Airport in Sri Vijaya Puram has direct flights from Chennai, Kolkata, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Visakhapatnam, and connections from most Indian cities. Morning flights from Chennai and Kolkata are the shortest (about 2 hours).

By sea

Government passenger ships sail from Chennai, Kolkata and Visakhapatnam to Sri Vijaya Puram. The voyage takes 3–4 days, and schedules are announced a few weeks ahead.

Between islands

Private cruises and government ferries connect Sri Vijaya Puram, Swaraj Dweep and Shaheed Dweep; the road runs north to Baratang, Rangat and Diglipur. See all routes

Permits & ID

Paperwork, simplified

  • Indian citizens don’t need a permit for Sri Vijaya Puram, Swaraj Dweep, Shaheed Dweep, Baratang or Diglipur — carry a government photo ID for flights, ferries and hotels.
  • Some protected spots (such as Jolly Buoy, Cinque Island and marine national park areas) need entry permits — we arrange them with your itinerary.
  • Foreign nationals receive a Restricted Area Permit on arrival. Rules change from time to time; check the official Andaman tourism website before travel.
  • Tribal reserve areas and the Nicobar Islands are closed to tourists.
What to pack

Your island checklist

  • Light cotton clothes, a hat, sunglasses and reef-safe sunscreen.
  • Swimwear, flip-flops and a quick-dry towel.
  • Mosquito repellent and any personal medicines (pharmacies are limited outside the capital).
  • A waterproof phone pouch and a power bank.
  • Some cash — ATMs and card machines are scarce on Neil and in the north; mobile data is patchy away from towns.
Questions

Travel FAQs

How many days are enough for the Andamans?

Five to seven days covers Sri Vijaya Puram, Swaraj Dweep and Shaheed Dweep without rushing. Add two to four days for Baratang, Rangat, Diglipur or more time on the beaches.

Is the Andaman Islands safe for families and solo travellers?

Yes — the islands are calm, friendly and well connected around the main tourist circuit. Follow lifeguard flags on beaches and the instructions of activity operators.

Can non-swimmers do water activities?

Absolutely. Sea walk, glass-bottom boats and semi-submarines need no swimming, and try-dive scuba is done with an instructor holding you throughout.

Do ferries get cancelled?

Occasionally, in rough weather. We re-confirm every sailing a day before and reshuffle your itinerary if needed — another reason not to schedule a crossing on your arrival day.

What is the food like?

Fresh seafood, North and South Indian, Bengali and continental dishes are easy to find. Vegetarian and Jain meals can be arranged at most hotels on request.
